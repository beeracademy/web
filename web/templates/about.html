How to play

Academy is a turn-based game. Each player draws a card, and the card has a value ranging from 2 to 14. A beverage contains the amount of sips chosen at the beginning of the game, defaulting to 14.

Before a player can draw a card, they have to be at the indicated value of their chosen beverage. When they reach that value, one of the other players draws a card for them.

If the drawn card is an ace, a new beverage has to be consumed and timed. The timer starts when the player begins drinking and stops when the beverage is finished.

There are many ways to win the game, with the most common being to consume the most sips. Other wins include drinking the most aces, the fastest ace, or the fastest average round time.
